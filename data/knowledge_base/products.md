The demo product includes:

- Workspaces for organizing a team's work.
- Project boards for tracking tasks and their status.
- Task assignments, due dates, and comments.

## Inviting a teammate

For this demo product, a workspace owner or administrator can invite a teammate:

1. Open the workspace.
2. Select **Workspace Settings**.
3. Open **Team**.
4. Select **Invite members**.
5. Enter the teammate's work email address and submit the invitation.

If the invitation option is missing, the customer may not have the required workspace role or plan access. Do not promise that a specific plan includes invitations. Ask a human support agent to review account-specific access.

Never ask a customer to share their password, authentication code, or access token.
