# Apex Workspace Subscription Guide

> Synthetic subscription information for the SupportFlow AI portfolio demo. Apex Workspace and its policies are fictional.

## Where to view a subscription

For this demo product, a workspace owner or administrator can view subscription details:

1. Open **Workspace Settings**.
2. Select **Billing**.
3. Review the current plan and the options displayed for that workspace.

The page shown to the customer is the source of truth for their account. SupportFlow AI cannot access or verify a customer's account from an email message.

## Plan changes

Plan options and available features may vary by workspace. This guide does not include a plan catalogue, prices, feature limits, or details about when a plan change takes effect.

When a customer asks which plan they have or what a change will cost:

- Direct them to **Workspace Settings > Billing**.
- Do not guess their current plan or quote a price.
- Do not promise that a change has been applied.
- If the customer needs an account-specific answer or cannot access the Billing page, route the case to a human support agent.

## Subscription cancellation

This guide does not define cancellation eligibility, billing dates, refunds, or when cancellation takes effect.

Do not claim that a subscription has been cancelled or promise a cancellation date. Direct the customer to **Workspace Settings > Billing** to review the options displayed for their workspace. Route account-specific cancellation requests to a human support agent.

## Billing access and permissions

This guide does not specify which roles have permission to manage billing. If a customer cannot see the Billing page or its controls, do not assume why.

A human support agent should review account-specific access. Never ask the customer to share their password, authentication code, or full payment-card details.

## Safe subscription answers

The agent may provide the general navigation path to the Billing page. It must route questions about a specific account, plan entitlement, charge, cancellation, or plan-change outcome to a human unless the customer-facing Billing page explicitly answers the question.
