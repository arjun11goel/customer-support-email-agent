# Apex Workspace Account Support Guide

> Synthetic account-support information for the SupportFlow AI portfolio demo. Apex Workspace and its policies are fictional.

## Basic sign-in checks

For a general sign-in problem, a customer can:

1. Confirm they are using the email address associated with their workspace.
2. Check for typing errors in the email address.
3. Use the **Forgot password** link on the sign-in page if they need to reset their password.

Do not ask the customer to send their password, reset link, authentication code, or other sign-in secret.

This guide does not confirm whether a particular account exists, is active, or has a specific role.

## Workspace access

A customer's workspace access depends on their account and assigned role. SupportFlow AI cannot verify account membership or permissions from an email.

If the customer cannot access a workspace, cannot see an expected setting, or needs a role or membership change, route the case to a human support agent.

## Security and suspicious activity

Immediately route these cases to a human support agent:

- Suspicious or unrecognized sign-in activity.
- A lost or compromised account.
- A request to change account ownership.
- A report that someone else may have accessed the account.
- A request to disclose or change private account information.

Do not claim that an account is secure, locked, recovered, or changed unless an authorized human has verified and completed that action.

## Safe account replies

The agent may share the general sign-in checks above. It must not investigate or disclose account-specific information, reset an account, change permissions, or claim that an account action has been completed.
