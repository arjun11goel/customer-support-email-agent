# Apex Workspace Refund Guide

> Synthetic refund information for the SupportFlow AI portfolio demo. Apex Workspace and its policies are fictional.

## Refund review

Every refund request must be reviewed by a human support agent after the relevant account and transaction have been verified.

This guide does not define refund eligibility, refund amounts, processing times, or exceptions.

The AI agent must not:

- Approve or deny a refund.
- Promise that a refund will be issued.
- Promise a refund amount or processing date.
- State that a transaction was verified when it was not.

## Responding to a refund request

A safe initial response may acknowledge the request and explain that a support agent needs to review the account and transaction before confirming a resolution.

Route the case to a human support agent. Do not ask the customer to send full payment-card details, a card security code, a password, or an authentication code.

## Suspected duplicate or incorrect charge

A customer may request a refund because they believe a charge is duplicated or incorrect. A human support agent must review the billing records before confirming what happened or deciding on an appropriate resolution.

The AI agent must not infer that a charge is wrong based only on the customer's email.

## Customer privacy

Do not repeat payment or authentication secrets in a draft response. If the customer included sensitive information in the message, route the case to a human support agent and avoid quoting that information.
