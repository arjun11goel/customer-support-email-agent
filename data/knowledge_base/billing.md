# Apex Workspace Billing Guide

> Synthetic billing information for the SupportFlow AI portfolio demo. Apex Workspace and its policies are fictional.

## Where to view billing information

For this demo product, a workspace owner or administrator can review billing information:

1. Open **Workspace Settings**.
2. Select **Billing**.
3. Review the invoices and subscription details shown for that workspace.

SupportFlow AI cannot inspect a customer's billing account from an email.

## Subscription charges

Customers are charged according to the subscription and terms selected for their workspace. This guide does not define exact prices, billing dates, taxes, trial periods, payment methods, or plan-change charges.

When asked about a specific charge:

- Do not guess what the charge is for.
- Direct the customer to **Workspace Settings > Billing** to review the account's invoice details.
- If the charge still needs explanation, route the case to a human support agent.

## Suspected duplicate charges

A customer may report that they were charged more than once. A support agent must compare the relevant account billing records before confirming whether a duplicate charge occurred.

The AI agent must not:

- Confirm a duplicate charge without account verification.
- Promise that a refund will be issued.
- Promise when a billing correction will happen.

Route suspected duplicate charges to a human support agent for review.

## Payment and account safety

Never ask a customer to send:

- A full payment-card number or security code.
- An account password or authentication code.
- An API key, access token, or other secret.

If a customer has already included sensitive information in an email, do not repeat it in a reply. Route the case to a human support agent.

## Safe billing answers

The agent may provide the general navigation path to the Billing page. Questions about a specific charge, payment status, invoice, tax, plan entitlement, or account must be reviewed by a human unless the customer-facing Billing page explicitly answers them.
